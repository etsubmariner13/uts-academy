    Getting to Know the Terms

    • Void-Hopping Fallacy: The error of assuming that an object, photon, or signal moves by leaping across empty gaps of nothingness, rather than propagating as a continuous shape-shift along an unbroken material medium.

    • Stationary Coordinate Track: A fixed row of physical grid intervals on the continuous material wire that does not move, stretch, or expand.

    • Kinematic Wave Re-Folding: The physical process of movement where a localized mass-knot uncoils its shape at one coordinate interval and re-forms at the adjacent interval without stretching the wire.

    • The Historical Anchor: Heraclitus of Ephesus (Fragments, B51, c. 500 BC), who proved that all apparent motion and physical transformation is a balanced, backward-turning attunement across an unbroken continuous medium, like the tension holding the bow and the lyre.
